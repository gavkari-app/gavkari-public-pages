Delete your PeekVahi account

PeekVahi is published by Gavkari Apps. You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled the app.

Request account deletion

Email a deletion request

You can also email gavkari.app@gmail.com with the subject Delete my PeekVahi account. Include the mobile number registered to your account so we can locate it.

How we verify your request

  1. We contact you by phone call or WhatsApp message on your registered mobile number to verify that you own the account. Keep passwords and one-time verification codes private.
  2. Once we verify ownership, we process your deletion request and email you when it is complete.

If you no longer have access to your registered number, tell us in your email so we can discuss how to verify your request.

What gets deleted

We delete your PeekVahi account information and associated farm records, including plots, crop cycles, work entries, expenses, and sales.

See the PeekVahi Privacy Policy for more about how we handle your information.
