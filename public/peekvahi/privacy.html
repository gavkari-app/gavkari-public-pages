PeekVahi Privacy Policy

Published by Gavkari Apps
Effective date: 20th November 2026

This policy explains how Gavkari Apps handles information when you use the PeekVahi Android app to manage your farm records.

Information PeekVahi uses

  • Account and setup information. When you set up PeekVahi, the app sends your name, mobile number, selected village, crop, plot name, and crop cycle start date to our service. The service uses account and device identifiers to connect your records with your account and manage your session.
  • Farm records. PeekVahi stores information you enter about crops, plots, farm work, expenses, and sales. This can include amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions.
  • Voice input. Where available, you can dictate text through your device’s speech recognition service. Depending on your device and provider, that service may process speech online under its own privacy terms. PeekVahi receives the recognized text. Text saved in a farm record or submitted during setup is handled like typed text.
  • Support messages. If you contact us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information you choose to provide so we can respond.

How we use and store information

We use account and farm information to set up your account, maintain your session, show your farm records, and synchronize changes. PeekVahi stores records on your device so you can work offline. The app sends records and changes to our service when it can connect. The production app communicates with our API over HTTPS.

Service providers

We use Render to host the PeekVahi API and Supabase to provide the production database. These providers process information to operate the service. Your device’s speech recognition provider may also process speech when you use voice input.

Retention and account deletion

We keep your PeekVahi account information and farm records until you request account deletion and we complete the request. Once we verify your request, we delete your account and its associated farm records from PeekVahi.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, visit the PeekVahi account deletion page. You can use that page after uninstalling PeekVahi. We use the information you provide to locate your account and verify your request.
